## SYSTEM OVERVIEW

### Equipment Slots
The player has **5 slots** at all times:

| Slot | Type | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| ⚔️ Weapon Slot 1 | Any weapon type | Primary — always active |
| ⚔️ Weapon Slot 2 | Any weapon type | Secondary — swap with a button |
| 🪖 Head | Helmet / Hood / Hat | DR + elemental resistance |
| 🧥 Body | Armour / Coat / Robe | DR + elemental resistance |
| 👢 Legs | Greaves / Trousers / Boots | DR + elemental resistance |

The player picks **any two weapon types** to carry simultaneously and swaps between them freely. A third weapon type found in the world can replace either carried slot.

### Weapon Types
| Type | Tiers | Range | Style |
|------|-------|-------|-------|
| ⚔️ Sword | 3 | Melee | High damage, close range, reliable |
| 🗡️ Throwing Sword | 3 | Ranged | Infinite throws, no return, lower per-hit damage |
| 🪃 Boomerang | 3 | Ranged arc | Hits on way out AND back, returns to hand |

### Damage Reduction (DR)
- Flat percentage reduction on all incoming damage
- Stacks across all three armour slots
- **Hard cap: 60% total DR**
- Each slot contributes: Head (max 10%) + Body (max 30%) + Legs (max 20%)

### Elemental Resistances
Reduce damage from specific hazard types. Stack across slots.
| Symbol | Type | Main Source |
|--------|------|------------|
| 🔥 | Fire / Heat | Forge, lava, heat zones |
| 🌊 | Water / Cold | Ocean, floods, tidal hazards |
| ⚡ | Void / Shadow | Sanctum, void entities, mirror traps |
| ✨ | Celestial | Spire, star sentinels, Aurelion |
| 🪨 | Physical | Blunt impacts, constructs, falling |

## ⚔️ SWORDS — 3 Tiers

Swords are the highest per-hit damage weapon. Short range — must be adjacent to the target. Specials reward aggressive, close-quarters play.

---

**Damage:** 4 hits to kill a standard enemy
**Speed:** Medium
**Special:** None — clean, reliable baseline.
**Appearance:** A straight-bladed short sword of decent iron. New enough to be sharp, plain enough to be uninteresting. This is a tool, not an identity.
**Flavour text:** *"A serviceable blade. It has not yet decided what kind of sword it wants to be."*

## 🗡️ THROWING SWORDS — 3 Tiers

Throwing swords fly in a straight line, deal damage on impact, and vanish — but the supply is **infinite**. A new blade appears in the player's hand immediately after each throw. Lower per-throw damage than melee swords; compensated by range and the ability to attack safely from a distance or across gaps.

The throwing sword is the ideal companion weapon to the sword: equip both, close in with the melee sword, switch to throwing for retreating enemies or platform gaps.

---

**Range:** 6 tiles

**Appearance:** A slim, balanced throwing blade — more javelin than sword, but clearly bladed. Plain iron. No frills.

## 🪃 BOOMERANGS — 3 Tiers

The boomerang travels in a wide arc away from the player and returns to hand automatically. It deals damage **on the way out and on the way back** — every enemy it passes through takes a hit. The arc width increases with tier. The boomerang cannot be thrown again until it returns.

This makes the boomerang a crowd control and positioning weapon — the player throws it to hit multiple enemies, ducks behind cover while it travels, and catches it on return. Excellent paired with a melee sword: throw the boomerang to soften a group, close in with the sword.

**Arc behaviour:** The boomerang travels forward in a slight curve (adjustable by throw angle), reaches its apex (4–8 tiles forward depending on tier), and curves back. Any enemy in its path on either leg of the journey takes damage. The return path mirrors the outward arc.

---

### 🪃 BOOMERANG TIER 1 — Carved Boomerang
**Damage:** 5 hits to kill a standard enemy (per pass — out counts as one hit, back counts as one hit, so a clean arc through one enemy deals 2 of those 5 hits)
**Arc Range:** 4 tiles forward
**Arc Width:** Narrow — approximately 2 tiles wide at apex
**Return Time:** 1.5 seconds
**Special:** None — learn the arc, learn the timing.
**Appearance:** A simple carved wooden boomerang, edges reinforced with iron strips. Clearly handmade. Warm-coloured wood, smooth from use.
**Flavour text:** *"Someone in the Mosshaven Wilds carved this from a fallen branch and decided throwing it was more interesting than not throwing it. They were correct."*

### 🪃 BOOMERANG TIER 2 — Ironwing
**Damage:** 4 hits to kill a standard enemy (per pass)
**Arc Range:** 6 tiles forward
**Arc Width:** Medium — approximately 3 tiles wide at apex
**Return Time:** 2 seconds
**Special:** **Stagger** — any enemy struck by the Ironwing is staggered for 0.5 seconds (brief pause in their attack or movement). Stagger applies on both the outward and return pass. Effective for interrupting enemy attacks mid-animation.
**Appearance:** A boomerang of hammered iron — industrial, angular, heavier than it looks. The edges are factory-sharp. The Vault's engineering mark is stamped on the flat.
**Flavour text:** *"Manufactured in the Ironclad Vault as a non-lethal restraint tool. Effective for staggering. Ironically, quite lethal if thrown correctly. The manual describes it as 'dual purpose.'"*

### 🪃 BOOMERANG TIER 3 — Celestial Ring
**Damage:** 3 hits to kill a standard enemy (per pass)
**Arc Range:** 8 tiles forward
**Arc Width:** Wide — approximately 5 tiles wide at apex. Can sweep an entire room on a clean throw.
**Return Time:** 2.5 seconds
**Special:** **Star Trail** — the Celestial Ring leaves a brief trail of star-light along its entire arc path. Any enemy that walks through the trail within 3 seconds of the throw takes 1 hit of damage. The trail fades after 3 seconds. On a wide throw through a crowded room, the trail can deal as much damage as the ring itself.
**Appearance:** A smooth ring of pale star-metal — no grip, no flat, just a perfect circle. Fits over the hand when not in use. Glows very faintly. Spins with a soft hum when thrown.
**Flavour text:** *"The Celestial Merchant sold this to Mira on her third visit to the plateau. She left it behind when she left the Spire. It was on the weapon rack beside the Starforged Blade. Both were waiting."*

## RECOMMENDED TWO-SLOT COMBINATIONS

These are natural pairings — not enforced by the game, but coherent combat styles:

| Style | Slot 1 | Slot 2 | Playstyle |
|-------|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Aggressive** | Sword | Boomerang | Close in with sword, throw boomerang into crowds before engaging |
| **Safe** | Throwing Sword | Boomerang | Full ranged — never close distance, arc and lance from safety |
| **Burst** | Sword | Throwing Sword | Melee primary, throwing sword for retreating or ranged enemies |
| **Crowd** | Boomerang | Throwing Sword | Boomerang sweeps groups, throwing sword picks off stragglers |

## MAX DR BUILD (REFERENCE)

If the player collects the highest-tier item in every armour slot:

| Slot | Item | DR | Resistances |
|------|------|----|------------|
| Head | Star Helm | 10% | ✨ 25% |
| Body | Constellation Plate | 30% | ✨ 30%, 🔥 10% |
| Legs | Star Greaves | 20% | ✨ 20%, 🔥 10% |
| **Total** | | **60%** | ✨ 75%, 🔥 20% |

At 60% DR, every hit deals 40% of its original damage. The cap ensures the game remains challenging even fully geared — enemies in Level VIII deal enough base damage that 40% is still meaningful.
